Dear Beloved Congregation and Friends,

"Sing to the Lord a new song; sing to the Lord, all the earth. Sing to the Lord, bless His name; proclaim the good news of His salvation from day to day." — Psalm 96:1-2

Praise the Lord! Welcome to the Maranatha Visvasa Samajam Songbook (మరనాత విశ్వాస సమాజము).

Music and songs are the language of praise, adoration, and prayer. Through every hymn and spiritual song, we lift up the holy name of Jesus Christ, remember His sacrifice on the Cross, and celebrate His everlasting grace.

మరనాత విశ్వాస సమాజము (Maranatha Visvasa Samajam)
ప్రేమ - త్యాగం | For God & Country

Whether you are worshipping in our church service, at fellowship gatherings, in family prayer at home, or in personal devotion, may these songs draw you closer to His presence.

Let everything that has breath praise the Lord!
May God's richest blessings, joy, and peace be with you and your family.

In His love and service,
Maranatha Visvasa Samajam
